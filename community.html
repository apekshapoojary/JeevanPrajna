Senior Financial Defense

Protecting Seniors from Digital, Banking & Pension Fraud

Cyber fraudsters specifically target senior citizens with fake urgency and technical jargon. Follow these non-negotiable rules:

1. The "Digital Arrest" Scam

The Trap: Someone calls claiming to be from Mumbai Police, CBI, or Customs stating your Aadhaar or parcel is linked to illegal money laundering, demanding you stay on Skype/WhatsApp video call and transfer money for "verification".

Truth: Real police or judicial agencies NEVER conduct trials or demand funds on video calls. Immediately disconnect and dial 1930.

2. The "Electricity Bill / Pension Disconnection" SMS

The Trap: SMS stating "Your electricity will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Call officer at 98XXXXXXXX" or "Your pension Life Certificate is suspended, click link to update".

Truth: Official government utilities never send mobile phone numbers for bill payments. Never click links or call unknown numbers from SMS.

3. AnyDesk / TeamViewer Screen Sharing

The Trap: A caller posing as bank customer support asks you to download "QuickSupport", "AnyDesk", or "RustDesk" to resolve a bank issue.

Truth: These apps allow callers to see your entire smartphone screen, passwords, and banking OTPs. Never install apps instructed by phone callers.

Golden Rule: The 2-Hour Golden Window

If you or an elderly family member accidentally enters bank details or shares an OTP, immediately dial 1930 within 2 hours.

The National Cyber Financial Fraud Reporting System can freeze the transaction in the inter-bank gateway before the fraudster withdraws the money.
