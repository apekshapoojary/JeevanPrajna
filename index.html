Important Government Update

Universal Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Cover for All Seniors 70 Years & Above

Under the landmark central government expansion, every Indian citizen aged 70 years and older is eligible for up to ₹5,00,000 per year in free hospitalisation coverage on a family floater basis, regardless of their family income, caste, or economic status.

  • A separate Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card is issued to eligible beneficiaries aged 70+.
  • Covers pre-existing diseases from Day 1 at all empannelled public and private hospitals.
  • Requires only an Aadhaar card with verified date of birth for registration via the beneficiary.nha.gov.in portal.

Clinical Wellness

Common Age-Related Conditions in India

Practical, non-technical guidance prepared in line with ICMR Geriatric Clinical Guidelines and AIIMS geriatric medicine advisories.

View Health & Medicine Handbook
Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

Target blood pressure for most seniors above 60 is generally around 130–139/80–89 mmHg. Avoid abrupt standing up to prevent orthostatic dizziness and falls.

Check weekly with validated digital arm cuff Read Guidance
Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

Hypoglycemia (low blood sugar below 70 mg/dL) is far more dangerous than mild hyperglycemia in seniors. Learn the 15–15 rule: 15g quick sugar and recheck in 15 mins.

Target HbA1c: 7.0% – 7.5% for seniors Read Guidance
Osteoarthritis & Knee Pain

Affects nearly 60% of Indian seniors. Gentle quadriceps strengthening, low-impact walking, safe footwear, and non-narcotic pain management protect joint mobility.

Avoid long periods of continuous sitting Read Guidance
Memory Loss & Dementia

Normal age-related forgetting is misplacing keys; dementia is forgetting what keys are used for. Early clinical screening at district hospital memory clinics slows decline.

Social engagement cuts risk by 40% Read Guidance
Fall Prevention at Home

Falls are the leading cause of hip fractures and loss of independence. Installing bathroom grab bars, non-slip rubber mats, and nightlights prevents 75% of domestic falls.

High risk areas: Bathrooms & stairs Home Safety Checklist
Cataracts & Vision Care

Cataract surgery is free under the National Programme for Control of Blindness (NPCB) at government hospitals and district vision centres. Yearly dilated eye exams are vital.

Annual screening prevents avoidable blindness Read Guidance

IGNOAPS — National Old Age Pension

Monthly social assistance for BPL elderly aged 60+ (₹200/mo by Centre + State top-up typically raising it to ₹1,000–₹2,500/mo across states).

Are You Caring for an Elderly Parent at Home?
