Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Universal ₹5 Lakh Health Cover for Seniors 70+

Under the landmark government expansion, all Indian citizens aged 70 years and above receive free secondary and tertiary hospitalisation coverage up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year on a family floater basis, regardless of income, caste, or wealth. Beneficiaries receive a dedicated Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card.

Annual Benefit
Up to ₹5,00,000 per family/year (cashless hospitalisation)
Eligibility Criteria
Any Indian citizen aged 70+ (determined strictly by Aadhaar DOB)
Empannelled Hospitals
30,000+ public and private empannelled hospitals nationwide
Pre-Existing Diseases
Covered from Day 1 with zero waiting period

Step-by-Step Registration Procedure:

  1. Visit the official portal beneficiary.nha.gov.in or download the Ayushman App on an Android smartphone.
  2. Enter your mobile number, authenticate with OTP, and select the Senior Citizen 70+ enrollment tab.
  3. Enter the senior's Aadhaar number and perform Aadhaar e-KYC via Mobile OTP or Face Authentication in the app.
  4. Take or upload a clear live photo and verify the permanent address.
  5. Once approved (usually within 15–30 minutes), download and print the Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card.

Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

A fundamental non-contributory social security pension for destitute elderly citizens living below the poverty line (BPL). The Central Government provides a core contribution, which state governments top up significantly to reach total monthly pensions ranging from ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 depending on the state.

Monthly Pension
₹1,000 – ₹2,500/month (Central core ₹200–₹500 + State Top-up)
Eligibility Criteria
Age 60 years or above belonging to a BPL household
Required Documents
Aadhaar card, BPL ration card/SECC census certificate, bank passbook, age certificate
Where to Apply
Block Development Office (BDO), Municipal Ward Office, or Common Service Centre (CSC)
Guaranteed Sovereign Savings Ministry of Finance 8.2% p.a. Interest
India Post SCSS Rules

Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)

Backed by the Government of India, SCSS is the safest and highest-yielding fixed-income avenue available to Indian senior citizens. Interest is credited automatically into the senior's savings account on a quarterly basis, offering dependable cash flow for retired living.

Current Interest Rate
8.2% per annum (compounded & paid quarterly on 1st April, July, Oct, Jan)
Deposit Limits
Minimum ₹1,000 | Maximum ₹30,00,000 (Individual or Joint with spouse)
Tenure
5 years (extendable by an additional 3-year block)
Tax Benefit
Investment eligible for Section 80C rebate up to ₹1.5 Lakh

Opening an SCSS Account:

You can open an SCSS account at any Department of Posts (Post Office) branch or major public and private sector banks (SBI, PNB, BoB, Canara, HDFC, ICICI). Submit Form-1, 2 passport photos, PAN card, Aadhaar card, and retirement superannuation proof if aged 55–60.

Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY)

RVY provides high-quality physical aids and assisted-living devices completely free of cost to senior citizens suffering from age-related disabilities or physical infirmities (vision impairment, hearing loss, teeth loss, or locomotor disability). Manufactured by ALIMCO (Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India).

Free Equipment Provided
Digital hearing aids, wheelchairs, walkers, spectacles, dentures, elbow crutches, walking sticks with stool
Eligibility Criteria
Senior citizens (age 60+) belonging to BPL category or with monthly income below ₹15,000
How Identified
District assessment camps organized by ALIMCO & District Social Welfare Officer
Required Documents
Aadhaar card, BPL card / Income certificate from Tehsildar, Medical disability certificate from government doctor
Tax Exemption Income Tax Department
Income Tax e-Filing

Special Income Tax Deductions for Senior & Super Senior Citizens

The Income Tax Act provides significant statutory exemptions to alleviate financial burdens for Indian seniors aged 60+ (Senior Citizens) and aged 80+ (Super Senior Citizens).

Section 80D (Health Expenses)
Up to ₹50,000 deduction for health insurance premiums or actual medical expenditure for senior citizens
Section 80TTB (Interest Income)
Up to ₹50,000 exemption on interest earned from bank savings, fixed deposits (FD), and post office deposits (No TDS up to ₹50k)
Exemption from Advance Tax
Seniors with no business income are exempt from paying advance tax (Section 207)
Section 80DDB (Specified Diseases)
Up to ₹1,00,000 deduction for medical treatment of specified chronic illnesses (cancer, renal failure, neurological conditions)

Maintenance & Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007

A powerful central law enacted specifically to safeguard the dignity, property, and physical survival of Indian senior parents. It creates dedicated, fast-track Maintenance Tribunals headed by Sub-Divisional Magistrates (SDMs) without requiring expensive civil court lawyers.

Monthly Maintenance
Adult children / legal heirs are legally bound to pay monthly maintenance to dependent parents (failure leads to imprisonment)
Revocation of Gifted Property
Section 23: If a senior transferred property to children on the condition of care, and care is refused, the transfer is declared null & void
Summary Eviction of Abusive Kin
Parents can order the immediate eviction of abusive adult children from self-acquired or ancestral houses
Punishment for Abandonment
Section 24: Leaving a senior parent with intention to abandon is a cognizable criminal offense punishable with jail time

How to File an Application at the Maintenance Tribunal:

  1. Draft a simple letter on plain paper addressed to the Maintenance Tribunal (Sub-Divisional Magistrate / SDM Office) of your local district.
  2. State the details: your age, address, relationship with children/heirs, details of property, and the refusal of maintenance or physical harassment.
  3. No advocate or court fee stamp is required. The Tribunal is mandated to resolve applications within 90 days.
  4. For emergency assistance with drafting, call the National Elder Line at 14567 for free field-officer intervention.
